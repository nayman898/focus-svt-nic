At roughly 0.90 MAF volts, reported airflow remains around 6.1 g/s in each major part of the run, rather than showing an obvious runtime change in the logged volts-to-airflow relationship. This is a narrow-band sanity check, not validation of the entire MAF curve or the MAFia hardware.

Steady raw FRPS is about 2.78 V in these windows (about 40 psi using the repo conversion), pump command about 25-26%, and PCM voltage about 13.2-13.3 V. These do not show a sustained steady-state fuel-pressure collapse; they do not rule out injector or reference-hose faults. The AEM channel falls below 8 AFR during startup and again at 239.253-251.909 s after restart. Those floor-like startup readings are excluded from fuel-correction sizing; the controller's warmup/status and raw output need checking before treating them as actual AFR.

LTFT, injector pulse width, purge command and actual bypass airflow are missing. The channel labeled commanded lambda closely tracks the STFT arithmetic, so it is not treated as independent proof of a deliberately lean base target. Negative STFT alone cannot size a MAF correction without understanding the other corrections. [HP Tuners fuel-trim definitions](https://www.hptuners.eu/help/vcm_editor_tuning_how_to_advanced_ve.htm)

## v010 decision

Do not issue MAF or blanket fuel edits from this recording: a fixed 20% fuel reduction based on the middle segment would lean out the near-zero-STFT segments, and LTFT / injector pulse width / EVAP purge are needed to identify the post-restart trim shift.

However, inspecting the `UJAHAS3` (`SVTv29.26`) parameter inventory revealed two hidden limiters directly driving the post-catch stall with a VTA BOV:
- `ECM 32945` (`Idle Dashpot Max Clip vs. VSS`) hard-clips dashpot airflow to `0.10 lb/min` at `0 / 10 / 15 mph`, neutralizing `v009`'s `0.80–1.00 lb/min` dashpot pre-position during parked tests and low-speed stops.
- `ECM 44582` (`Spark Idle Control Minimum`) allows idle spark to drop to `-6.00°` during the post-catch rich rebound right before the lean snap.
- `ECM 2291` (`Maximum RPM to Disable DFCO`) is `750 RPM` (below idle target on `> 35 mph` moving decels).

`v010` is therefore prepared as a ready-to-build 4-edit return-to-idle recovery trial on `v009` (`ECM 32945` `0/10/15 mph` `0.10 -> 0.25 lb/min`, `ECM 44582` `-6.00° -> +4.00°`, `ECM 44218/44217` `950 -> 1,050 RPM`, and `ECM 2291` `750 -> 1,100 RPM`), while keeping MAF untouched and requiring `Bank 1 LTFT`, `Injector Pulse Width`, and `EVAP Purge` in the `v010` log.
