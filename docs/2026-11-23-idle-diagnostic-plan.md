## Capture sequence

1. **Before starting.** Save stored and pending DTCs and any freeze frame. Note the flashed filename, cold start versus warm restart, fuel, and any changes since the last capture. Keep the saved fault data for comparison after the run. [HP Tuners pre-tune diagnostics](https://docs.hptuners.com/VCM_Editor/Introduction/Getting_Started/)
2. **Key-on through warm idle.** Begin recording before crank. In neutral with the parking brake set, hold the same clutch and accessory state through each test. Mark the start, any failed crank or rough running, first closed-loop entry, and any fan or load change. Once warm and settled at roughly **175–190 °F ECT**, log **30 seconds of idle**. Mark two or three AEM gauge readings and their times or record a short parked video of the gauge and Scanner.
3. **Controlled releases.** Hold **1,750 RPM for about 10 s**, release fully, and record **20 s** after release. Proceed to **2,100 RPM** only after the first return is clean. Note the gauge reading during the steady hold as a second comparison point. A clean return stays **at or above 800 RPM** and settles within **75 RPM of target in 5 s** without a second dip or hang. End the progression after a failed catch. Follow the [repo abort criteria](../AGENTS.md), including a stall or severe dip below **650 RPM**. Keep this capture parked and out of boost.
4. **Save the evidence.** Read codes again after the run. Provide the native **HPL**, full **CSV** with interpolation off, the flashed filename, event times, gauge readings or video, and any codes or freeze frame. A good starting name is `nic-v006-parked-diagnostic`. If built and tested, save `v007` as a separate capture under comparable ECT, spark mode, and accessory state. [HP Tuners log markers](https://docs.hptuners.com/VCM_Scanner/Introduction/Changelog/)

## What to do with the result

- **Gauge and Scanner disagree at steady RPM.** Correct the input, ground reference, units or transform first. If both agree and still read lean while B1S1 switches near zero trim, verify the AEM sensor and bung, check for air ingress before the AEM, and compare with an independent wideband. A misfire can also leave extra oxygen in the exhaust. Start with the fault data and plug, wire, and coil checks if the engine stumbles or a cylinder is flagged.
- **RPM falls as the PCM removes bypass air before the catch.** Start with `v007`’s two decay edits from the exact `v006`. If the first drop persists, try `v008` from the same `v006` with more initial airflow around 2,150 RPM. Compare minimum RPM, settling time and any hang. If IAC duty is already rising early while RPM continues to drop, check the valve and bypass passage, voltage and load changes before more airflow edits. Use `v006` as rollback if a candidate worsens recovery. [v007 decay](../tunes/v007-idle-handoff-README.md) · [v008 pre-position](../tunes/v008-low-rpm-dashpot-catch-README.md)
- **Releases are clean but a stationary accessory load still pulls idle down.** Record A/C engagement or the fan cycle and check the idle-air response and charging voltage. If the hardware responds normally and a higher idle target is needed for margin, a separate **950 → 1,000 RPM** trial is reasonable in the active target field, ECM **44218** for Park/Neutral or **44217** for Drive. Verify which target the PCM uses. [Idle speed references](02-VCT-IDLE-AND-FANS-README.md#2-idle-speed--spark-damping-ecm-44218-44217-47996)
- **The stumble persists once the idle catch and AEM are verified.** Capture the symptom under gentle, steady, no-boost load with gear, speed, and spark mode documented. Look for fuel pressure sag paired with rising pump duty, a repeatable spark transition, or cylinder-specific misfires. If measured lambda and repeatable positive trim both support a real lean condition, compare idle and light-load MAF voltage bands and check for unmetered air before sizing a fuel change. Leave boost validation until the idle recovery and light-load stumble are resolved and the pressure and wideband channels are reliable.

Before moving to a rolling test, verify repeatable parked catches after a warm restart and through ordinary accessory load, and reconcile the AEM gauge and Scanner. Use a shop or closed area for the first low-speed, no-boost checks. Log clutch-in stops and low-speed maneuvering, and stop if the undershoot or stumble returns. Use those results and any hardware diagnosis to decide whether it is ready for traffic.
